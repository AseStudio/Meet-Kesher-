How Kesher works

From setting up your first session to staying in touch between them — here's the whole flow.

1

Create a session

Set up a session as a host and get a shareable link. No complicated setup — you're ready to invite people in a couple of taps.

2

Share the link

Send it to whoever you're meeting with — a class, a job candidate, an interview, a get-together. They join with the link, no separate account wrangling required on their end.

3

Meet live

Real-time video and audio for everyone in the session, built to hold up on ordinary connections — quality adjusts automatically so the call keeps going even when bandwidth doesn't cooperate.

4

Stay connected after

Community channels keep the conversation going between sessions, so the relationship doesn't reset to zero every time the call ends.

Recording sessions

Hosts on a paid plan can record a session from a desktop browser. Once the session ends, the recording is ready to preview and download right from the summary screen.

Plans and payment

Kesher has a free tier plus paid Pro, Max, and Premium plans with more session time and features. When you upgrade, you can choose to be billed monthly or pay once for 30 days with no ongoing commitment — and pay by card or Mobile Money, whichever's easier.
