Kesher means connection. Around here, that's not a tagline — it's the moment a host hits start and the people they teach, coach, or meet with actually show up for it.

A class. An interview. A coaching call. A get-together. All live, all on video, with a whiteboard everyone can actually draw on and a raised hand that doesn't get lost in a grid of tiles. Session's full? The waitlist grabs the next open seat itself, the moment one opens — nobody has to sit there refreshing.

Sessions end. The relationship doesn't have to. Community keeps it going in between — a host runs a channel around what they teach, drops updates and notes, and can pull the whole group into a live session in one tap.

This isn't built for a call you'll forget by tomorrow. It's built for the class that keeps meeting, the mentor you keep coming back to — for people you see more than once. A place, not a link that gets lost in your inbox.
