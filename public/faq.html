Frequently asked questions

What is Kesher?

Kesher is a live video platform for hosting sessions — classes, meetings, interviews, or casual get-togethers — with community channels built in to keep the conversation going between sessions.

Do I need to create an account?

Hosting a session requires an account. Joining one someone shared with you is designed to be as low-friction as possible.

What plans are available?

Kesher has a free tier to get started, plus Pro, Max, and Premium plans that add more session time and features like recording. See current pricing for each tier in the app.

How does billing work — monthly or one-time?

Your choice, for every plan. "Billed monthly" auto-renews each month. "Pay once" charges you a single time for 30 days of access with no auto-renewal and no ongoing commitment — upgrade again whenever you like.

Can I pay with Mobile Money?

Yes, alongside card payment. Mobile Money is available for one-time ("Pay once") purchases. Recurring monthly subscriptions are card-only, since auto-renewal needs a payment method that can be safely charged again automatically, which Mobile Money doesn't currently support.

Can I record my sessions?

Yes, if you're on a paid plan and hosting from a desktop web browser. After the session ends, your recording is ready to preview and download from the summary screen.

Is my data used for advertising?

We don't sell your personal information to third parties. Our public marketing pages display ads through Google AdSense, which may use cookies to serve and measure those ads — see our Privacy Policy for the full details.

How do I get help or ask something else?

Reach out through the contact details in our Terms of Service, or from within the app once you're signed in.
