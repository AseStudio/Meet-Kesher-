Privacy Policy — Kesher

Privacy Policy

Kesher — Video Sessions & Collaboration

1. Overview

This policy explains what information Kesher collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. By creating an account or joining a session, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

Account information — when you create an account or sign in with Google, we collect your name, email address, and (optionally) phone number.

Session data — session titles, codes, chat messages, reactions, poll responses, whiteboard content, and files you submit during a session.

Usage & device data — basic technical information (browser type, connection quality) needed to run video calls reliably, and, if you enable notifications, a push-notification subscription token.

Guest participants — if you join a session as a guest without an account, we collect only the name (and optional email) you provide for that session.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, and let hosts and attendees find and join sessions
  • To operate live video, audio, chat, whiteboard, and file-submission features during sessions
  • To send session-related notifications (e.g. a lobby is about to expire)
  • To enforce host moderation actions, including bans, within the app
  • To maintain and improve the reliability and security of the service

4. Third-Party Services

Kesher relies on the following providers to operate. Each processes a limited slice of your data solely to provide their part of the service:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage
  • Agora — real-time video and audio transmission during sessions
  • Google — optional "Sign in with Google" authentication

We don't sell your personal information to third parties, and we don't use your data for advertising.

5. Data Retention

Account and session data is retained for as long as your account is active. Chat messages and session history are tied to the sessions you've participated in. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 7).

6. Data Sharing Within Sessions

Your display name is visible to the host and other attendees of any session you join. Session hosts can see your mic/camera status, chat messages you send, and files you submit. Direct messages sent within a session are only visible to the sender and recipient.

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • Access or update your profile information from within the app
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data

To make a request, contact us at the email below.

8. Children's Privacy

Kesher is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "last updated" date below.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at rubenassensoh@gmail.com.
